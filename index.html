How it plays

One thumb, one road, one more run.

Every run is a fresh stretch of road. The longer you last, the faster it gets.

Traffic

Thread the gap

Steer with your thumb, hold gas to go faster and score more, and brake when the gap closes.

  • Cars, trucks and fire engines in every lane
  • Barriers, cones and pedestrians on the crossings
  • Shield, Nitro and Magnet power-ups
Risk pays

Close calls score big

Every near miss earns bonus points. Chain them within a few seconds to multiply up to ×5, and a near miss at a truck or fire engine counts double.

Six worlds

From city lights to the open desert

The road runs through city, suburb, forest, western, arctic and wasteland, and day turns to night as you drive.

Garage

Build the car you want to crash

Nine cars, from the Family Wagon to the Apex GT, each with its own perk. Every car keeps its own upgrades and style.

  • Engine, gearbox and tyre upgrades
  • Paint, livery, wing, roof, number plate, wheels, neon, tail lights and boost trail
  • Try any style on before you spend
Every day

Something new every day

Come back for a reward every day. Day seven gives an item you cannot buy.

  • Daily Challenge: the same road and car for everyone, three medals to win
  • A new event every Monday, such as Rush Hour, Big Rigs or Night Drive
  • Road Pass: a free 30-tier season with weekly missions

Always something to chase

Missions, rivals and a fair deal.

35 missions

Daily goals and long-term missions that unlock styles. Pin one and watch its progress live while you drive.

Two leaderboards

Every run scores on distance, speed and risk. Sign in to Game Center for the all-time High Scores and a fresh Daily Challenge board every day.

Fair by design

No in-app purchases and no ads between runs. Every ad is your choice: spin the prize wheel, keep driving once after a crash, or keep your streak after a missed day.

Your garage comes with you

Signed in to iCloud, your progress is backed up to your own iCloud and comes back on a new iPhone or iPad. Reminders are optional, at most one a day.
