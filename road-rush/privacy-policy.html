Road Rush Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Road Rush is a game for iPhone and iPad published by Kasper Andersen. This policy explains what happens to your data when you play. The short version: the game has no accounts and no server of its own, and your progress stays on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account. The optional ads come from Google, and Google collects data to provide them.

Your game progress

Your cars, upgrades, credits, records and settings are saved on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, the game also keeps a copy in your own iCloud account, so your progress comes back on a new device. That copy is stored by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. Your progress is not sent to me. To delete it, use Reset Progress under Settings in the game, which also clears the iCloud copy.

Ads

Road Rush shows optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. You only see an ad if you choose to watch one: to spin the prize wheel, to keep driving once after a crash, or to keep your reward streak after a missed day. To have an ad ready, Google's software starts when you open the app, so it can collect data even if you never watch an ad.

According to Google's own privacy declaration, this software can collect:

  • your approximate location, based on your IP address
  • device identifiers
  • advertising data, such as which ads you have seen
  • how you interact with ads and the app
  • crash, performance and other diagnostic data

Google uses this data to show and measure ads, for analytics and to prevent fraud. I do not receive information that identifies you. Road Rush does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, so the app does not give Google access to Apple's advertising identifier.

If you are in a region where consent is required, such as the EEA or the UK, Google's consent form is shown in the app. You can change your choice at any time under Settings > Ad privacy. Read more about how Google uses data from apps that use its services and in Google's privacy policy.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your best score and your best Daily Challenge score of each day are sent to Apple's Game Center and shown on the leaderboards under your Game Center profile. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. You can play without signing in.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders, the game schedules at most one local notification a day on your device. Nothing is sent to a server to do this. You can turn reminders off under Settings in the game or in the iOS Settings app.

Sharing a run

The Share button opens the standard iOS share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you choose a destination.

Children

Road Rush is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Your rights

If you live in the EEA or the UK, you have rights over your personal data, including access, correction, deletion and objection. Because I hold no data about you, ad data requests are best sent to Google through the links above. You can also complain to your national data protection authority. In Denmark that is Datatilsynet.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub. Issues are public, so please do not include personal details.
