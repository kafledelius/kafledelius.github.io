Road Rush Support

Need help with Road Rush, or found a bug? Open an issue on GitHub. Please include your iPhone or iPad model and what happened. Issues are public, so please do not include personal details.

How do I play?

Steer with the left side of the screen, hold gas to go faster and score more, and brake when you need room. Avoid traffic, barriers and pedestrians on the crossings.

Why am I not on the leaderboard?

Sign in under Settings > Game Center on your device. Scores are sent when you beat your best run, and Daily Challenge scores go to that day's leaderboard.

How do I change my ad choices?

Where consent is required, open Settings in the game and choose Ad privacy.

How do I start over?

Open Settings in the game and choose Reset Progress. This permanently deletes your cars, upgrades, credits and records, including the copy in your iCloud.

Will my progress come to a new iPhone or iPad?

Yes, if you are signed in to the same iCloud account on both devices. Open the game on the new device, and the copy with the most progress is used.

How do I turn reminders on or off?

Open Settings in the game and choose Reminders. Road Rush sends at most one reminder a day.
